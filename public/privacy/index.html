2. What we collect

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or any other personal information. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools.

  • Technical connection data. To show you up-to-date content, the app requests it from our servers. As for any internet service, the servers and hosting providers process technical data such as your IP address, date/time and the type of request, for security and to deliver the content. We do not use it to identify you.
  • Calendar. When you tap “Add to calendar”, the app opens your phone’s own calendar screen with the event details. The app does not read your calendar and nothing is sent to us.
  • Maps, phone, email and websites. Buttons such as Map, Call, Email and Website open other apps or websites. Their own privacy policies apply from that point.
  • Editors’ area (web only). People authorised to update the content sign in at /admin with email and password. We process that email address and an encrypted password hash only to authenticate them and keep the content safe.

3. Legal basis

Delivering the content and keeping the Service secure is our legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); for the editors’ area, performance of the agreement with the editor (art. 6(1)(b)).

4. Who processes data for us

Hosting and database: Supabase (data region: EU, Ireland). Website hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). These providers act as processors and may transfer data outside the EEA under the safeguards required by GDPR (such as Standard Contractual Clauses). We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

5. Retention

Technical logs are kept by the providers for the short periods set in their own policies. Editor accounts are kept while the person is authorised and deleted on request.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect their data.
