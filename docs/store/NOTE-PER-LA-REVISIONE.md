# Note per i revisori (App Store Connect → "App Review Information" / Play Console → "App access")

**Accesso:** l'app NON richiede login né account. Nessuna credenziale da fornire.

**Testo da incollare (inglese):**
```
WAOOO is an information guide to Milan Fashion Week (calendars, runway shows, presentations,
events, trade shows, showrooms, brands, stores). No sign-in is required and no demo account is needed.

Content is loaded from our backend and edited by our editorial team through a separate web page
that is not part of the app. The app never reads the user's calendar: "Add to calendar" opens the
system event editor with the event details pre-filled, and the user decides whether to save it.
"Map", "Call", "Email" and "Website" open the corresponding system apps or the browser.

The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking and no user-generated content.
Events marked "By invitation only" or "By appointment only" are informational: the app does not sell
tickets or grant access.
```

**Contatto per la revisione:** nome, telefono ed email del titolare (obbligatori in App Store Connect).

**Perché non è un "semplice sito" (linea guida 4.2):** navigazione nativa, ricerca globale, salvataggio eventi nel calendario di sistema, apertura mappe/telefono/email, contenuti aggiornati dal backend e disponibili offline come copia di riserva.
