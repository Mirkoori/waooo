**Description:**
```
WAOOO unlocks Milan Fashion Week.

Find what is happening, where and when — and save it to your calendar in one tap.

FASHION WEEK
• Fashion Weeks: the calendar of the international weeks
• Runway: shows, day by day, with time, address and access notes
• Presentations: collections presented by appointment
• Events: parties, talks and openings
• Trade Shows: fairs and exhibitions for buyers

MARKET
• Showrooms: sales campaigns, dates, addresses and the brands on show; email or call to book an appointment
• Brands: every brand and where to see it
• Stores: where to buy the collections, with opening hours and directions

INFO
• Contacts and the companies that support WAOOO

Search everything from one place. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Event details can change and many events are by invitation or appointment only: always check with the organiser.
```
